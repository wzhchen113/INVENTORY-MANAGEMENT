### Should-fix
- `src/store/useStore.ts:3983-3991` — comment accuracy in the `recordExportedOrder` header. The spec 176 edit inserted a third clause into the parenthetical ("...no longer nulls it — a same-store reload keeps it, spec 175 — and a late previous-store foreground result is now dropped, spec 176 R-1 — but those two paths remain."). "Those two paths" was written when it meant the two bullet causes (a mid-export store switch nulls it; a foreground load for a new as-of date replaces it). With two "no longer" clauses now sitting right before it, a reader will take "those two" to mean the self-echo and the late result, which is the opposite of what is true. Reword to "but the first two paths remain" (or name them: "a store switch and an as-of-date change still do").
- `src/store/useStore.ts:2375-2379` — the `finally` comment gives the wrong reason for the invariant: "The bump sits after the session probe, so the newest load always reaches this reset." What guarantees the newest load reaches `finally` is that the bump is adjacent to `try` with no await or early exit between them (the precise statement is at L2153-2158). "After the probe" is why the two bail paths and out-of-order probes cannot strand the gates, which is a different claim. Suggest: "The bump is opened right before `try` (see L2153), so the newest load always reaches this reset." Keep the "accepted residual" sentence as is.
